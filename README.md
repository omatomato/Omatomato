## Independent security research, OSINT & digital investigations

I turn scattered public signals into timelines, evidence maps, technical records, and decisions that can be checked.

My work focuses on cybersecurity, fraud tracing, incident triage, privacy, and source protection. Public material is deliberately redacted: the goal is to demonstrate method without exposing clients, active cases, raw evidence, or live indicators.
